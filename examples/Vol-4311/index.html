Table of Contents

  • Preface
    Miguel Areias, Vítor Santos Costa, Ricardo Rocha
    This volume collects the papers accepted for publication at five workshops and the Doctoral Consortium associated with the 42nd International Conference on Logic Programming (ICLP 2026). The events took place in Lisbon, Portugal, on July 20-23, 2026. There were 57 papers submitted for peer-review to the workshops and the Doctoral Consortium included in this volume. After a thorough review process, 55 papers were accepted for publication, 44 of which were presented at the workshops and 11 at the Doctoral Consortium. Of the 44 workshop papers presented at workshops, 36 were regular papers, 6 were short papers, and 2 were abstracts corresponding to invited talks. The Doctoral Consortium papers were all short papers.
